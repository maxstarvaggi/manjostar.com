Fasting Tracker Pro

Track your intermittent fasting journey with advanced analytics — a product of Manjo Star's software team.

Overview

A comprehensive Android application designed for those practicing intermittent fasting. Track your fasts, monitor progress, and achieve your fitness goals with detailed analytics and insights.

Key Features

  • Intuitive fasting timer with real-time updates
  • Advanced analytics dashboard
  • Custom fasting schedules and goals
  • Progress tracking and statistics
  • Local data storage for privacy
  • Beautiful and responsive UI

Benefits

Maximize your intermittent fasting results with Fasting Tracker Pro. Monitor your patterns, stay consistent, and achieve your health goals through data-driven insights and motivational tracking features.
